This week: a 30-inch fish out of Baffin, the Galveston jetties turning on at the top of the tide, a wade-fishing walkthrough for the Lower Laguna grass flats, and the one number you need to know before you keep a trout this fall.

## conditions · Galveston jetties: the last two hours of incoming is the window

Guides on the North Jetty are seeing the bite line up with the top of the flood — clean green water pushing in, bait stacked on the channel side. Slackwater's forecast has the southeast wind easing to 8 mph by midweek.

## conservation · Fall trout: know the slot before you keep one

TPWD's statewide spotted seatrout rules are still the tighter post-freeze numbers — check the current bag and slot before your trip, and remember the oversize fish is a once-a-year tag, not a daily allowance. CCA Texas has a printable card. Handle big fish in the water, and skip the lip grip on anything you're releasing.
